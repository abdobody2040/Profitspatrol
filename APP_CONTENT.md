## 2. Arcade Games Hub (50 Unique Games in 8 Districts)

The **Games / Play Hub** features 50 bespoke mini-games categorized into **8 Districts**, each reinforcing key lessons through active mechanics.

### District 1: 🟡 Money District (Stations 01–10, Free/Intern Tier)
1. **Coin Catcher (`coin`)**: Sort tumbling pennies, nickels, dimes, and quarters into appropriate bins before the timer expires.
2. **Needs vs Wants (`needs`)**: Swipe cards left or right to classify everyday items into survival needs versus optional luxury wants.
3. **Coin Sorter (`sorter`)**: High-speed matching game organizing mixed change into standard bank rolls.
4. **Piggy Budget (`budget`)**: Drag allowance tokens into Savings, Spending, and Giving jars to satisfy weekly budget goals.
5. **Super Savings (`savings`)**: Avoid impulse shopping hazards while steering Ollie toward a high-interest savings vault.
6. **Change Master (`change`)**: Act as a cashier and compute change for cash transactions in real-time.
7. **Expense Detective (`detective`)**: Spot hidden or recurring subscription leaks in a family financial ledger.
8. **Discount Dash (`discount`)**: Rapidly calculate 10%, 25%, and 50% discount tags on clearance shelf items.
9. **Cashflow Runner (`cashflow`)**: Endless runner balancing income speed boosts against liability speed traps.
10. **Bank Maze (`maze`)**: Guide your vault cart safely through security lasers to deposit cash reserves.

### District 2: 🟢 Builders District (Stations 11–20, Founder Tier)
11. **Lemonade Stand Tycoon (`lemonade`)**: Balance ice, sugar, and lemon inventory according to changing weather forecasts.
12. **Snack Shack (`snack`)**: Assemble healthy lunches to order while minimizing ingredient spoilage.
13. **T-Shirt Designer (`tshirt`)**: Screen-print trendy custom shirts, set retail prices, and fill bulk orders.
14. **Pet Care Service (`pet`)**: Time management grooming and walking pets to maximize customer ratings.
15. **Cafe Rush (`cafe`)**: Manage barista orders, keep coffee beans freshly stocked, and delight morning commuters.
16. **Ad Agency (`adagency`)**: Pick optimal color palettes, taglines, and billboard slots to market client goods.
17. **Customer Quest (`customerquest`)**: Handle diverse customer service scenarios to turn upset clients into brand fans.
18. **Sales Master (`salesmaster`)**: Interactive dialogue simulator choosing persuasive sales angles.
19. **Brand Builder (`brandbuilder`)**: Design logos, pick fonts, and establish brand guidelines that match target vibes.
20. **Marketing Mix (`marketingmix`)**: Optimize the 4 Ps (Product, Price, Place, Promotion) for product debuts.

### District 3: 🔵 Risk & Wealth District (Stations 21–25, Founder Tier)
21. **Invest Island (`investisland`)**: Allocate resources between safe coconut farms and volatile pearl diving ventures.
22. **Risk Radar (`riskradar`)**: Scan startup ventures and identify warning flags like high debt or weak moats.
23. **Portfolio Quest (`portfolioquest`)**: Balance stocks, bonds, gold, and cash to withstand economic shocks.
24. **Compound Mountain (`compoundmtn`)**: Watch an investment snowball roll down a hill, gaining speed over time.
25. **Scam Detective (`scamdetective`)**: Identify phishing emails, fraudulent prizes, and deceptive investment pitches.

### District 4: 🟣 Operations District (Stations 26–30, Founder Tier)
26. **Time Tycoon (`timetycoon`)**: Organize calendar blocks using the Eisenhower Matrix (urgent vs important).
27. **Team Builder (`teambuilder`)**: Draft a balanced squad of designers, coders, and marketers with complementary traits.
28. **Supply Chain Challenge (`supplychain`)**: Route cargo ships, trains, and delivery vans around weather storms.
29. **Inventory Master (`inventorymaster`)**: Practice Just-in-Time inventory without stocking out of hot holiday items.
30. **Business Crisis (`businesscrisis`)**: React to PR disasters and supply shocks with decisive contingency choices.

### District 5: 🟠 Innovation District (Stations 31–35, Founder Tier)
31. **Idea Factory (`ideafactory`)**: Combine two unrelated concepts (e.g. drone + pizza) to pitch novel innovations.
32. **Product Lab (`productlab`)**: Test prototype materials to balance build cost with structural durability.
33. **Prototype Rush (`prototyperush`)**: Build an MVP in under 60 seconds with limited spare components.
34. **Customer Feedback (`customerfeedback`)**: Analyze user survey reviews to prioritize the next feature update.
35. **Innovation Challenge (`innovationchallenge`)**: Solve eco-friendly packaging puzzles with zero-waste materials.

### District 6: 🔷 Leadership District (Stations 36–40, Tycoon Tier)
36. **Negotiation Arena (`negotiationarena`)**: Negotiate wholesale supplier contracts to establish win-win pricing.
37. **Team Captain (`teamcaptain`)**: Boost morale and reassign overwhelmed teammates during tight crunch deadlines.
38. **Deal Maker (`dealmaker`)**: Structure partnership terms with international retailers.
39. **Communication Lab (`communicationlab`)**: Translate confusing corporate jargon into crystal-clear instructions.
40. **Leadership Quest (`leadershipquest`)**: Lead your company through ethical dilemmas (profit vs community impact).

### District 7: 🟦 Strategy District (Stations 41–45, Tycoon Tier)
41. **Market Wars (`marketwars`)**: Compete against rival businesses for market share in a simulated city grid.
42. **Strategy Empire (`strategyempire`)**: Turn-based business simulation expanding stores across multiple regions.
43. **Competitor Hunt (`competitorhunt`)**: Reverse-engineer competitor advantages and discover underserved niches.
44. **Growth Engine (`growthengine`)**: Optimize customer acquisition funnels (awareness, consideration, purchase).
45. **Business Tycoon (`businesstycoon`)**: Balance multiple business divisions simultaneously to generate peak EBITDA.

### District 8: 💖 Launch & Scale District (Stations 46–50, Tycoon Tier)
46. **Sales Master X (`salesmasterten`)**: Advanced corporate enterprise sales negotiation against tough buyers.
47. **Marketing Machine (`marketingmachine`)**: Run multi-channel ad campaigns optimizing cost per click and conversion.
48. **E-commerce Rush (`ecommercerush`)**: Fulfill thousands of web orders while managing server load and warehouse dispatch.
49. **Pitch Battle (`pitchbattle`)**: Present your 90-second elevator pitch to a panel of tough angel investors.
50. **Startup Launch (`startuplaunch`)**: Take your startup from garage incubator all the way to a public stock IPO.

## 3. The CEO Library (Curated Books & Summaries)

The in-app library contains over 100 chapter-by-chapter summaries and interactive key takeaways adapted for young readers:

### Classic Foundation Books
- **Think and Grow Rich** (*Napoleon Hill*): The power of desire, mindset, and the Mastermind group.
- **Rich Dad Poor Dad for Teens** (*Robert Kiyosaki*): Assets put money in your pocket; liabilities take money out.
- **The Richest Man in Babylon** (*George S. Clason*): Pay yourself first, control expenditures, make gold multiply.
- **How to Win Friends and Influence People** (*Dale Carnegie*): Genuine interest, smiling, active listening.
- **The Psychology of Money** (*Morgan Housel*): Doing well with money is about behavior, not raw intelligence.
- **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective Teens** (*Sean Covey*): Proactivity, beginning with the end in mind, synergy.

### Modern Entrepreneurship & Innovation
- **Zero to One** (*Peter Thiel*): Building unique monopolies rather than copying existing competition.
- **Shoe Dog** (*Phil Knight*): The relentless early hustle behind creating Nike.
- **The Lean Startup** (*Eric Ries*): Build-Measure-Learn feedback loops and MVPs.
- **Atomic Habits** (*James Clear*): Getting 1% better every day through identity-based systems.
- **Originals** (*Adam Grant*): How non-conformists champion novel ideas.
- **Start with Why** (*Simon Sinek*): Inspiring teams through core purpose.

## 8. Economy, Subscriptions & Monetization Model

### Family & Individual Plans
- **Intern (Free Tier)**:
  - Access to Module 1–3 lessons & District 1 arcade games
  - 1 business property slot
  - 5 standard energy hearts (refills every 20 minutes)
- **Founder ($9.99/mo)**:
  - Unlimited Energy Hearts
  - Full access to 200 curriculum lessons
  - Access to Districts 1–5 arcade games (35 games)
  - 3 business property slots & offline progress
- **Board Member ($14.99/mo - Recommended)**:
  - Up to 3 child accounts connected to 1 Parent Dashboard
  - Detailed learning analytics, screen-time bounds, and chore allowances
  - All Founder tier perks
- **Tycoon ($89.99/yr)**:
  - Full access to all 50 games including Districts 6–8 (Leadership, Strategy, Launch)
  - AI Mentor "Ollie" voice assistant
  - Exclusive Gold CEO avatar skin and private island badge

### School & Educator Tiers
- **Teacher Solo (Free)**: Class management up to 35 students, gradebook, school-hours lock.
- **Teacher Pro ($49/yr)**: Unlimited classrooms, student progress exports, and custom homework assignment tracks.
- **School Small / Medium / Large ($299 to $1,499/yr)**: School-wide leaderboards, LMS sync (Google Classroom & Clever), and district admin reporting.

## 9. Age Modes (Junior vs Explorer) & Accessibility

1. **Junior Mode (Ages 6–9)**:
   - Simplified vocabulary with phonetic pronunciation hints.
   - Larger touch targets (min 64px).
   - Higher visual emphasis on tactile emojis and audio read-aloud via Ollie.
   - Simplified single-concept math problems.

2. **Explorer Mode (Ages 9–12)**:
   - Real-world business formulas (ROI, margins, unit economics, inflation rates).
   - Touch targets 48px+.
   - Multi-step case studies and portfolio risk modeling.

3. **Arabic RTL & Cultural Localization**:
   - Complete right-to-left layout alignment with native typography (Cairo & Baloo 2).
   - Culturally tailored currency equivalents and local business examples.
   - Correct Arabic punctuation placement (e.g. `؟` query marks).
