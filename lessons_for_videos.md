## Module: Money Basics

### Lesson MB_01: What is Money?

**Difficulty:** Level 1

**Script / Content:**
> Imagine trying to buy a PlayStation with a goat! 🐐 Before coins existed, people used the **Barter System**. This meant swapping stuff you had for stuff you needed. 'I'll trade you two chickens for those shoes!' It was messy, smelly, and very noisy. Money was invented to make trading way easier!

**Key Term:** Barter System

**Quiz Element (For Engagement):**
- *Question:* Before cash, how did people get what they needed?
- *Correct Answer:* By swapping items (Barter)
- *Wrong Options:* By using credit cards | By fighting for it | By asking nicely

**Flavor Text / Outro:**
*Trade complete! You swapped a goat for a game console. (Phew!)*

### Lesson ENT_16: Profit & Loss

**Difficulty:** Level 3

**Script / Content:**
> Math Alert! 🚨 **Profit** is the money you keep in your pocket. 

Formula: (Money from Sales) - (Cost of Ingredients) = PROFIT.

If you sell a cookie for $5 but it cost $6 to make, you have negative profit (a Loss). Oops!

**Key Term:** Profit

**Quiz Element (For Engagement):**
- *Question:* What is Profit?
- *Correct Answer:* Money left over after paying costs
- *Wrong Options:* The money you lose | The cost of ingredients | The name of the store

**Flavor Text / Outro:**
*You're in the green! You did the math and counted your profits.*

### Lesson NEG_192: Know Your BATNA

**Difficulty:** Level 1

**Script / Content:**
> Your **BATNA** (Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement) is your secret superpower. 💪 It's what you'll do if the deal falls through. If you're offered $8/hour but could get $10 elsewhere — $10 is your BATNA. The stronger your BATNA, the MORE power you have in any negotiation. Never negotiate without one.

**Key Term:** BATNA

**Quiz Element (For Engagement):**
- *Question:* How does a strong BATNA help you in a negotiation?
- *Correct Answer:* It gives you the confidence to walk away and more bargaining power
- *Wrong Options:* It lets you lie about offers | It guarantees you win | It speeds up the contract signing

**Flavor Text / Outro:**
*Walk-away power! Your competitor's offer made the original employer raise theirs instantly.*

### Lesson NEG_193: Anchoring

**Difficulty:** Level 2

**Script / Content:**
> The first number in a negotiation becomes the mental reference point — this is called **Anchoring**. ⚓ If a seller says '$100', every counter-offer feels relative to that. Smart negotiators ALWAYS anchor first and high. If you want $70 — ask for $90. The final number will land closer to your real target.

**Key Term:** Anchoring

**Quiz Element (For Engagement):**
- *Question:* You want to sell your bike for $80. What is the best anchoring strategy?
- *Correct Answer:* List it at $100 to leave room for negotiation
- *Wrong Options:* List it at $80 to be honest | Ask the buyer to price it first | Never mention a number first

**Flavor Text / Outro:**
*Anchor dropped! You listed at $120, settled at $95 — exactly what you wanted.*

### Lesson NEG_194: Active Listening

**Difficulty:** Level 2

**Script / Content:**
> The best negotiators are the best LISTENERS. 👂 **Active Listening** means truly hearing what the other person needs — not just waiting for your turn to talk. Ask questions: 'What matters most to you?' People often say they want money but really want respect or security. Find the real need and you find the deal.

**Key Term:** Active Listening

**Quiz Element (For Engagement):**
- *Question:* A client says they want a lower price. An active listener should first...
- *Correct Answer:* Ask what's driving the concern — it may not actually be about price
- *Wrong Options:* Immediately drop the price | End the negotiation | Ignore it and repeat your offer

**Flavor Text / Outro:**
*Heard and resolved! You discovered the client wanted faster delivery, not lower cost.*

### Lesson NEG_195: The Power of Silence

**Difficulty:** Level 2

**Script / Content:**

**Key Term:** Strategic Silence

**Quiz Element (For Engagement):**

- *Wrong Options:* Immediately lower your price | Leave the room | Apologize and start over

**Flavor Text / Outro:**
*Silence wins! You waited 15 seconds after your offer — and they accepted without pushback.*

### Lesson NEG_196: Win-Win Negotiation

- *Correct Answer:* Each side gets something more valuable than just a price compromise
- *Wrong Options:* Both sides end up with equal money | There are no losers in any deal | It always takes longer to negotiate

**Flavor Text / Outro:**
*Win-win achieved! Both companies grew revenue after your creative deal structure.*
