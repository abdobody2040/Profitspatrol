# Product Identity

ProfitsPatrol is a gamified business education platform for children and teenagers.

The product teaches:

* Financial Literacy
* Entrepreneurship
* Business Strategy
* Marketing
* Investing

through gameplay.

The product is NOT a school portal.

The product is NOT an LMS.

The product is NOT an admin dashboard.

The product is a Business Tycoon RPG.

---

# Core Design Goal

Every screen must answer:

"What can the player earn next?"

# Design Principles

1. Mobile First

Design for phones first.

Desktop is secondary.

---

2. Gamification First

Always prioritize:

* Rewards
* Progression
* Unlocks
* Collection
* Achievement

over traditional educational layouts.

---

3. Progress Visibility

Always display:

* XP
* Level
* PP Tokens
* Streak
* Progress

whenever possible.

---

4. Mission-Based Learning

Never present lessons as lessons.

Transform:

Lesson

Into:

Mission

Challenge

Quest

Adventure

Campaign

---

5. Visual Density

Use:

* Large cards
* Large buttons
* Large typography
* Clear rewards

Avoid:

* Tiny text
* Dense grids
* Data tables

---

# Mascot Rules

The owl is a product guide.

Use the owl to:

* Teach
* Celebrate
* Explain
* Motivate

The owl should feel alive.

---

# Animation Rules

Every major interaction should have motion.

Required:

* Hover
* Press
* Reward
* XP gain
* Achievement unlock
* Level up

Use Framer Motion.

---

# Color Rules

Use brand palette from the component library.

Favor:

Orange

Gold

Navy

Cream

Avoid:

Corporate gray

Enterprise blue

Flat white dashboards

---

# World Building Rules

The app should feel like a living business world.

Examples:

Lemonade District

Retail Town

Startup Valley

Real Estate Zone

Stock Market City

---

# Decision Framework

If multiple designs are possible:

Choose the option that feels more like:

A game

instead of

A dashboard.

Always optimize for:

Engagement

Retention

Excitement

Reward anticipation

Progression

Collection

Achievement

Never optimize for enterprise aesthetics.

---

# Technical Rules

Keep:

* Existing functionality
* Existing routes
* Existing state management
* Existing business logic

Redesign:

* Visual hierarchy
* UI components
* Layout
* Motion
* Gamification

Do not rewrite working systems unless necessary.

---

# Final Rule

Whenever generating UI:

Ask:

"Would a child believe this is a game?"

If the answer is no,

redesign it again.
