## 🏬 Deep Business Simulations (32+ Games & Freemium Gating)

In addition to the 50 neighborhood arcade stations, Profits Patrol features 32+ deep economic simulations powered by `GameEngine.tsx` and custom templates (retail, cooking, services, production, and trading).

### 🎟️ Freemium Gating Rules
- **Intern (Free Tier)**: Access to **3 free simulation games only**:
  1. `lemonade` / `BIZ_01_LEMONADE`: **Lemonade Tycoon** (`#SIM-01` — Retail & Food)
  2. `brand`: **Brand Builder** (`#SIM-02` — Creative & Events)
  3. `BIZ_02_PIZZA`: **Pizza Restaurant / Rush** (`#SIM-03` — Retail & Food)
- **Founder Tier**: Unlocks all standard and founder business simulations (`#SIM-04` onward: Cupcake Bakery, Coffee Cart, Taco Truck, Car Wash, Pet Salon, Dropshipping, Repair Shop, Factory, etc.).
- **Tycoon Tier**: Unlocks **100% of all simulations**, including exclusive executive simulations:
  - `BIZ_NEGOTIATION`: **Boardroom Battles / Negotiation Arena**
  - **High-End Corporate & Space Ventures**
